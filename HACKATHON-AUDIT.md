# Hindsight: hackathon audit

20 September 2026. Local changes only; no push, deployment, paid generation or messages to others.

## Recommendation

**Pitch an inspectable build decision, not a synthetic population.**

The strongest demo line is: “Before I build this, what would change my mind?” Freeze a scenario, change one input, reveal the assumptions, inspect the actual pilot, and export a next-test rule. This produces a visible artefact a generic chat does not automatically preserve. It is a focused workflow, not a claim that nobody else can build it.

Borrow the existing local prototype and recorded engine runs. Build only the missing comparison and decision record. That is what this audit did. Do not spend the remaining preparation time on more agents, another cinematic clip or another synthetic population.

## What is genuinely differentiated, and what is not

The category is established. [Minds](https://getminds.ai/guide/audiences) already offers reusable synthetic audiences, inspectable studies and comparisons. [Aaru](https://aaru.com/simulation) describes simulation with external validation. Both were checked during this audit. Neither company's validation establishes ours.

Our proposed focus is narrower: a founder records an assumption, its provenance and the real observation that would change a build decision. A saved baseline and a predeclared next test make the learning claim inspectable. This is a credible positioning choice; uniqueness, defensibility and demand for Hindsight remain unproved.

The demo should show a founder changing their next action, not treating a percentage as permission to build. The readiness offer is an example hypothesis. Travel-product demand and demand for Hindsight are separate tests.

## Remaining red flags that code cannot remove

1. **No real customer evidence.** The team must recruit and collect it. Do not substitute invented feedback. Even five discovery conversations are qualitative learning, not a conversion estimate.
2. **No demonstrated predictive accuracy or improvement.** Preserve frozen predictions and use compatible, independent observations. Revising from a response and reproducing that same response is not validation.
3. **Thin product differentiation.** The decision workflow is more focused, but competitors cover much of the category. Prove that a founder makes a better next-test decision using it.
4. **Final submission is unfinished.** Existing CapCut draft lacks current product footage and narration; old captions overclaim. The audit supplies recordable scripts, not a completed video.
5. **Publication state.** These changes are local and uncommitted. Check teammate edits and the actual submission branch before publishing; the audit did not silently merge or push another task's branch.
6. **Persistent local credentials.** `.env.local` and `.env.higgsfield.local` exist locally, are permission-restricted and Git-ignored. No key values were read into the report. Keep them out of a zip, screen recording and public commit. Ignoring a file is not encryption.
