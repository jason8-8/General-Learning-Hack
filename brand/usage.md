# Usage

Do: void slide, phosphor on one number, Fog body, Mute meta, one mark, short lines, one surface lift.

Don’t: gradients, gradient text, matrix rain, dark glow as atmosphere, radial halo behind the title, stock-photo heroes, nested cards, decorative eyebrows, more than two colors on one slide, Inter, extra families, pure `#000`, “probability of success” as a badge, titles in Space Mono.
