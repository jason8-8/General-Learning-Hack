# General Learning — Hackathon Intro

**Private notes:** Focus on the pitch. Track statements: Automate your studies / Automate your last internship or job / Automate your life.

## General Learning Overview

- Parent company behind Revision Dojo (IB), One Prep (SAT/ACT/APs), MasteryMe (UK GCSE)
- 1.5M users across 180 countries; team of 30-40 people
- YC-backed: Fall 2024 cohort
- 4th product teased: JetPads, aimed at university students

## Hackathon Schedule

- 9:00 AM: kickoff and introductions
- 10:00 AM: tracks released (5-minute window)
- 1:00 PM: lunch served; snacks and drinks available throughout
- 3:00 PM: showcase, pitches, and demos
- 4:00 PM: winners announced

## Track Statements

- Automate your studies: improve or automate a day in your student life
- Automate your last internship/job: tackle the most menial tasks from past work experience
- Automate your life: eliminate day-to-day administrative and brain-drain tasks
- Can tackle one track or combine multiple if creative enough

## Judging and Prizes

- Champion: sponsored entry to Hack the East
- 1st runner-up: $7,500; 2nd runner-up: $100
- $5,000 referral bonus if you refer someone who gets hired
- Judging criteria weights creativity heavily: no cookie-cutter, tutorial-based, or to-do list solutions
- Focus on the pitch: communicate why you built it and what value it delivers, not just the technical aspects
- Submission requirements:
  1. Public GitHub repo
  2. Two-minute demo video of the working solution
  3. Written description: what you built, the problem, and why your approach is best

## Hiring

- 4% global acceptance rate; every hire is top 1% of candidates
- Open to interns, grads, part-time, and full-time
- Prepare an elevator pitch: what makes you different, why hire you over an AI agent
