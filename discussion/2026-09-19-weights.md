# GL Hack — Idea refinement

**Private note:** Deterministic

## Concept Overview

- AI market validation tool: predicts how the market will respond to a product idea
- Core pitch: input an idea, get a scored market research report (likelihood of success, challenges, competitors, potential customers)
- Framed as a simulator, not just an agent doing generic research

## Two Core Components

- AI-generated synthetic consumer personas (sampling the target audience)
- Prediction model scoring whether those personas would adopt the product
  - Narrow the sample by audience type, geography, age, company profile
  - Example: voice agents for corporates vs. note-taking apps for students

## Deterministic Approach (Key Design Decision)

- Feedback from others: move away from pure LLM output toward a more deterministic, quant model
  - LLMs produce inconsistent results with the same prompt; also biased toward US data
- Quant model assigns numeric weights to factors (age group, company age, B2B vs. B2C, digital maturity, willingness to pay)
  - Example: a 2-year-old SaaS startup = 80% likely to adopt; a 50-year-old family business = ~5%
- Scoring system needs careful architecture to avoid hallucination

## Model Validation Strategy

- Use historical data to back-test the model (like a quant trading algorithm)
  - Input pre-2021 data, predict whether a known company succeeded, compare to reality
  - Proves model accuracy before using it for new ideas
- Deviation/confidence interval should also be surfaced in output (e.g. "40% chance of success +/- X%")

## Scope and Constraints

- Generalized model requires too much data across all industry types
  - Decision: narrow scope to one category (e.g. EdTech) for the hackathon demo
  - Can expand to other verticals later
- Key open questions:
  - How to define product similarity (features? positioning? e.g. Claude vs. GPT)
  - How to weight each factor in the scoring engine
  - How to calculate proxy rate against analog companies

## Work Split and Next Steps

- Research competitor prediction models (e.g. how Aaru does it)
- Define scoring engine input parameters: ICP fit, problem intensity, price fit, market segment, analog proximity
- Think through end-to-end UX: idea input -> model run -> market report output
- Reconvene in ~30 minutes
