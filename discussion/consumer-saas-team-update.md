# Team update and questionnaire

Status: team questionnaire prepared; teammate responses pending. [Current build plan and answer sheet](../product/BUILD.md#team-questionnaire).

We're fixing the direction around consumer SaaS, specifically note-taking and personal productivity. The founder enters an idea, reviews the interpreted brief and confirms it before assessment. Three research agents cover the market, competitors and customers in parallel. They feed shared evidence into our quant model and the planned MiroFish swarm, then the report explains prospects, barriers, overlooked angles and next tests.

The main decisions are in. Now we need everyone's input on how to build it. There's an 18-question team questionnaire in product/BUILD.md, with a recommendation and suggested respondents for each question. Please add your name, answer and a short reason to the answer sheet. Different views are useful; leave disagreements visible so we can resolve them.

Jason: focus on the example, success target, user flow and report. Quant/data: cover feature inputs, missing evidence and what we can validate. MiroFish/integration: cover the model connection, agent behaviour, runtime and fallback. Frontend: cover confirmation, report and comparison. Everyone: add your availability and the work you can own in Q18.

MiroFish is the selected direction, but we still need a real feasibility check. The quant calculation should stay inspectable, and synthetic reactions won't count as customer evidence. We haven't established a dataset that validates a probability of success.

Reply with the question number, your answer and one reason, or edit the table directly in GitHub. Mark anything you can't answer as needing investigation. We'll use the agreed answers to finalise the build rather than keep changing direction.
