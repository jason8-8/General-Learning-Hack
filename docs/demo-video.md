# Demo video plan

Draft storyboard, 19 September 2026. This proposes what to demonstrate; it does not approve every feature. Read alongside the [pitch deck](pitch-deck.md) and [build plan](build-plan.md).

## What the viewer should see

A founder or VC enters an idea, examines its commercial potential, checks the evidence behind one finding, then changes one assumption and compares the result. “Quantitative VC” is an explanatory analogy, not an agreed product name or a promise of investment returns.

Done means:

- One recorded run shows input → assessment → evidence → changed assumption → comparison.
- The viewer can distinguish sourced observations, model reasoning and unknowns.
- The ending states what the comparison taught us and what needs a real-world test.

## Proposed 90-second narrative

| Time | Screen action | Narrative intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10s | Opening promise slide. | Enter an idea and assess its commercial potential. Lead with the outcome, not a problem interview. |
| 10–20s | Enter one idea, target audience and price; run the assessment. Keep the selected geography visible. | Show how little input is needed and what assumptions the assessment uses. |
| 20–45s | Show potential buyers, relevant competitors or alternatives, and adoption factors. | Explain one commercially useful finding. Buyer segments are candidates, not confirmed customers. |
| 45–60s | Open the evidence and reasoning behind that finding. | Show the source, date, relevant observation, inference and limitation. |
| 60–80s | Change price OR audience, rerun, and compare with the baseline. | Demonstrate which finding changes, why, and what stays uncertain. Change only one variable. |
| 80–90s | Closing slide. | State what was learnt and the next test. |

Total: **90 seconds = 20 seconds of slides + 70 seconds of product**. Two slides frame a continuous product demonstration. There is no five-slide problem deck.

The idea, category, geography and price are still to be selected. EdTech is an option. Choose an example with accessible evidence and a meaningful single-variable comparison; do not invent market findings to fit the storyboard.

## Working behaviour and fallback

The form must accept the shown inputs, the assessment must use them, the evidence panel must resolve actual source records, and comparison must preserve both runs. The edited assumption must reach the assessment logic. A second hard-coded screenshot does not demonstrate this behaviour.

Prefer an evidence pack gathered and checked before recording, with live assessment and comparison over that pack. Display “Cached evidence” and the retrieval date whenever it is used. Cached evidence is a disclosed data source, not proof of live web research.

If live retrieval is included and fails, switch visibly to the checked pack. If assessment itself fails, a saved run may be shown as “Previously generated result”, but that does not pass the working edit-and-compare check. Fix or reduce scope before recording the final submission. Disclose any cuts that remove waiting time; don't imply an unmeasured response speed.

## Official submission wrapper

The official requirement is a **two-minute video including a working prototype**. The 90-second narrative is the core, not an assumption that a shorter upload qualifies. Proposed two-minute edit:

| Video time | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 0–90s | The complete narrative above, opening with the promise. |
| 90–105s | Brief use context: founders testing an idea and VCs assessing an early proposition; state the problem addressed. |
| 105–120s | Actual technologies, prototype limits and closing identification. Complete this only after the build is known. |

This wrapper adds video context, not extra pitch slides. No separate deck or live-pitch duration is specified in the supplied rules.

## Recording checks

- Rehearse the full path with readable text, visible clicks and one finding explained clearly.
- Verify every factual claim against its cited source. Synthetic personas alone are not evidence.
- If a score appears, label it a transparent heuristic and expose its factors; never call it a probability of startup success.
- Capture a clean run and a backup; listen to the exported audio and watch the entire two-minute file.
- Protect recording, export and submission time using the [build-plan milestones](build-plan.md#ordered-milestones).
