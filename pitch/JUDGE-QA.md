# Judge questions and next test

## Why not just use ChatGPT?

```text
A chat can help brainstorm. Our prototype keeps the source evidence, scenario assumptions, original result and next-test rule together. You can change one input, see what changed and export the decision. The model is replaceable; the decision record is the product focus.
```

## Doesn't Minds or Aaru already do this?

```text
Yes, synthetic audience research is an established category. Our focus is the founder's next build decision: which assumption matters, what evidence supports it, and what real result would change the plan. We haven't proved a market advantage over those products yet.
```

## Are these 1,000 real AI interviews?

```text
No. The presentation contains 1,000 authored scenario rows. Its charts are calculated from explicit assumptions. We separately completed two actual local-model runs of twenty synthetic profiles, and show those results with their provenance.
```

## How accurate is it?

```text
We haven't established predictive accuracy. Our real pilot chose free tools at both prices, so it didn't demonstrate price discrimination. The next step is to freeze predictions and compare them with independently collected traveller choices on the same offer.
```

## Does it learn automatically from customers?

```text
The lab preserves a baseline and supports a versioned revision using illustrative feedback. No real feedback or improvement on independent evidence has been demonstrated. That's a validation task still ahead of us.
```

## Who pays for Hindsight?

```text
Our first proposed user is a founder deciding whether to build a consumer product. A paid study or founder subscription is a business-model hypothesis. We haven't tested willingness to pay for Hindsight itself.
```

## Next real test

Proposed, not completed: recruit five people with upcoming multi-city trips for discovery. Show the same concrete sample deliverable and price beside their actual free alternatives. Ask for a choice first, then reasons; do not show synthetic results. Record recruitment and sample limits. Five conversations can expose missing objections, not estimate a conversion rate.

Before recruitment, freeze the offer, price, outcome definition and decision rule. If the offer changes, treat it as a new experiment. Reserve a later, separate group for evaluating any revised audience. The team must collect the responses; no invented interview stands in for them.

Also test Hindsight with founders: ask them to use the receipt to choose a real next action. Traveller demand tests the example offer; founder usefulness tests this product. These are different questions.

## Research checked in this audit

- [Minds audiences](https://getminds.ai/guide/audiences): reusable audiences and inspectable studies; warns against treating synthetic outputs as automatic population estimates.
- [Aaru simulation](https://aaru.com/simulation): existing research/simulation/validation workflow. Do not claim the learning loop itself is novel.

The distinction is a proposed focus and implementation choice, not an exclusive capability claim.
