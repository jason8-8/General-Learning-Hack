# Demo video — 120 seconds

Record this. Do not invent findings to fill the beats.

## Shape

90 seconds of product, 30 seconds of wrapper.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10s | Slide 1. Promise. | Enter an idea. Assess its commercial potential. |
| 10–20s | Form: idea, audience, price. Geography visible. Run. | This is all we assume. |
| 20–45s | Buyers, alternatives, adoption factors. | One useful finding. Candidates, not customers. |
| 45–60s | Evidence panel for that finding. | Source, date, observation, inference, limit. |
| 60–80s | Change **price or audience**. Compare to baseline. | What moved, what did not, what is still unknown. |
| 80–90s | Slide 2. Learning. | What changed. What to test next in the real world. |
| 90–105s | Context. | Founders testing a pitch; VCs scanning an early idea. |
| 105–120s | Stack and limits. | Actual tools used. Cached evidence labelled. No success probability. |

## Rules while recording

- Change only one variable on the rerun.
- Cached evidence stays labelled “cached” with a date.
- If assessment fails, do not pass a frozen screenshot off as a live compare.
- A score, if shown, is a **heuristic**. Never “chance this startup works.”
- Synthetic personas are hypotheses. They are not proof of demand.
