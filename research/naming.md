# Naming decision

Decided: 20 September 2026.

The product is named **Hindsight**, replacing the working name **general-learning-hack**. Jason confirmed the choice in the naming discussion.

Use **Hindsight** as the canonical spelling and capitalisation in the product, presentation and brand materials.

The rationale is to give founders the perspective they usually gain after launching, before they commit to building. The product remains a consumer SaaS launch-assessment tool; the name does not imply validated predictions.

This decision selects the product name. Domain and trademark availability have not been checked.
