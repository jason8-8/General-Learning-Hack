## Grammar and fingerprint

Custom grammar: Evidence trail. Persistent case navigation and an actual comparison receipt, a fixed-copy introduction, freely accessible working chapters, ending with a note and export. Bans scroll hijacking, automatic changes to input values, fake completion indicators, giant pinned text acts and hidden controls.

The eight stock grammars do not fit unchanged: filmic and continuous-world prevent direct access; editorial/poster remove the approved illustrated hero; gallery/cutlist lose the decision sequence; split-stage imposes a misleading winner; live-surface forbids the already approved marketing hero. We retain the approved opening while explicitly defining tool-first chapter navigation and an input/export ending.

Registry is empty: no previous row to compare. Four device families: parallax, boundary reveal, flow entrance and sticky comparison hold. Never animate data values merely because of scroll.
